FRACS Study

A personal study-document tool for one user.

What this application is

FRACS Study is a private, single-user tool built and operated by David Zula, a surgical trainee, for personal exam preparation. It is not a commercial product, is not offered to the public, and has no sign-up.

There is exactly one user: the owner of the Google Account that authorises it. No other person can access it.

What it does

The tool compiles study summaries for topics on the Royal Australasian College of Surgeons (RACS) General Surgery curriculum. For each topic it:

  • researches the topic across public reference sources and locally stored textbooks and course notes;
  • generates a formatted study document (Microsoft Word) held on the owner's own computer;
  • saves that document to the owner's own Google Drive, so it is available on the owner's other devices.

It also reads the owner's own Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Contacts so those items can be surfaced alongside study schedules. Nothing is read on behalf of anyone else.

Why it needs Google authorisation

Google Drive access is used for one purpose: placing the study documents the tool has produced into a folder inside the owner's Drive. Gmail, Calendar and Contacts access is read-only or owner-directed, and is never used to contact third parties.
